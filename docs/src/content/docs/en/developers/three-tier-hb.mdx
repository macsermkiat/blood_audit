`bba.deterministic_classifier.classify` applies the implemented RBC rules in a fixed order. Equality at an indication threshold qualifies. Hb at or below 7.0 g/dL is `APPROPRIATE` before MTP, unknown-context routing, or the higher-threshold review checks.

This page describes code behavior. Clinical approval of the policy and its use remains a separate responsibility. The deterministic classifier does not emit `INAPPROPRIATE`. It routes unresolved indication judgments to review.

## Order-level exits

The classifier first checks the returns ledger. A complete `not_transfused` disposition yields `RETURNED_NOT_TRANSFUSED`, unless structured intraoperative transfusion or blood loss of at least 500 mL contradicts it.

With `DECLARED_USE_PREOP_EXEMPT_ENABLED` on, declared `surgery` or `type_screen` use then yields `PERIOP_TRANSFUSION_EXEMPT`. Both exits precede Hb interpretation and are excluded from appropriateness scoring. MSBOS reservation assessment can separately produce a reservation finding or review result.

## Hb precedence

After the order-level exits, the first matching rule wins:

| Order | Condition | Result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | No usable Hb, with the missing-Hb option off. | `INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE`. |
| 2 | Hb ≤ 7.0 g/dL. | `APPROPRIATE`, including `UNKNOWN` and non-threshold cohorts. |
| 3 | Assigned cohort is `MTP`. | `APPROPRIATE`. |
| 4 | Assigned cohort is `UNKNOWN`. | `NEEDS_REVIEW`. |
| 5 | Legacy procedure routing is enabled and a procedure is within the prior 6 hours. | `APPROPRIATE`. |
| 6 | Legacy routing has an upcoming procedure within 72 hours or declared surgical use, and Hb is above the cohort threshold. | `NEEDS_REVIEW`. |
| 7 | A qualifying delta-Hb window fires. | `APPROPRIATE`. |
| 8 | The remaining cohort has no numeric threshold. | `NEEDS_REVIEW`. |
| 9 | Hb is at or below the cohort threshold. | `APPROPRIATE`, unless hemodilution or the single-low-Hb flag requires review. |
| 10 | Hb is above the threshold and below 10 g/dL. | `NEEDS_REVIEW`. |
| 11 | Hb is at least 10 g/dL. | `POTENTIALLY_INAPPROPRIATE`. |

The global 7.0 rule is inclusive. The high-Hb route remains `POTENTIALLY_INAPPROPRIATE` at exactly 10.0. MTP and delta-Hb can return earlier, so high Hb alone does not determine the result.

`enable_missing_hb_positive_evidence` defaults to false. When explicitly enabled, missing-Hb MTP can clear, hard perioperative evidence can clear outside unknown context, and unresolved cases defer to review. This option changes the missing-Hb branch, not the numeric thresholds.

Legacy procedure routing runs only when `DECLARED_USE_PREOP_EXEMPT_ENABLED` is off. The upcoming-procedure branch uses Hb strictly above the cohort floor. A reservation alone does not auto-clear an indication.

## Cohort thresholds

<HbTierMatrix />

Names in the table are `CohortLabel` members. Stored values use names such as `ortho_surgery` and `cardiopulmonary_comorbidity`.

| Cohort | Indication boundary | Context |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `DEFAULT` | Hb ≤ 7.0 g/dL. | No higher-priority cohort matched. |
| `CARDIAC_SURGERY` | Hb ≤ 7.5 g/dL. | Recent qualifying cardiac or major vascular operation. |
| `ORTHO_SURGERY` | Hb ≤ 8.0 g/dL. | Qualifying orthopedic operation. Cardiac history is not required. |
| `ESRD_EPO` | Hb ≤ 7.0 g/dL. | ESRD diagnosis plus dialysis-context medication. |
| `CARDIOPULMONARY_COMORBIDITY` | Hb ≤ 8.0 g/dL. | Heart-disease diagnosis. Lung disease alone does not qualify. |
| `MTP` | No numeric threshold. | The assigned MTP cohort bypasses later Hb tiers. |
| `HEME_MALIGNANCY_ACTIVE` | No cohort threshold. | Hb ≤ 7.0 clears globally. Remaining cases require context review. |
| `UNKNOWN` | No cohort threshold. | Hb ≤ 7.0 clears globally. Remaining cases require review. |

`ORTHO_CARDIAC` is retained for historical records. The current detector emits `ORTHO_SURGERY` for orthopedic surgery alone.

## Cohort assignment is a separate sequence

The detector first checks whether procedure events are unavailable. Unavailable events produce `UNKNOWN`. Available but empty events can fall through to `DEFAULT`. It then checks MTP, orthopedic surgery, cardiac surgery, active hematologic malignancy, heart-disease comorbidity, ESRD with medication, and default, in that order.

This differs from classifier precedence. The classifier puts an already assigned `MTP` before `UNKNOWN`, but the detector cannot assign MTP after its missing-procedure-data exit. Keep those two sequences distinct when building context or interpreting a trace.

The MTP detector uses the prior one-hour blood-order window. The qualifying pattern is at least four RBC units, or RBC with FFP and platelet co-ordering. Surgical cohorts use qualifying operative events in the preceding 30 days.

## Lab trends and review flags

The Hb lookup accepts analytic-valid results from 2 to 25 g/dL at or before the order and less than seven days old. It prefers hematology over POCT. A higher lab `item_no` breaks equal-time ties.

Delta-Hb can bypass the remaining tiers when a drop reaches 1.5 g/dL within 6 hours, 2.0 within 12 hours, or 2.5 within 24 hours. For Hb above 7.0 but at or below a higher cohort threshold, at least 2 L of crystalloid in four hours or an isolated low Hb without a prior 24-hour trend can require review.

Low blood pressure and high heart rate remain supporting evidence. They do not independently override this deterministic Hb sequence.

The implementation is in `src/bba/deterministic_classifier/classifier.py`. Cohort predicates are in `src/bba/cohort_detector/rules.py`. [Pipeline stages](/blood_audit/en/developers/pipeline-stages/) describes the response route after deterministic classification.
