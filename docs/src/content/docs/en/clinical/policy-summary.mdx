This page describes the rules implemented in the repository. It paraphrases the KCMH internal policy bundle, including PR 17.2 and the three-tier Hb specification. It is not a bedside transfusion guideline. A conflict between the implementation and an approved institutional policy needs clinical review before deployment.

## RBC thresholds

<HbTierMatrix />

The classifier uses these cohort thresholds in g/dL. Threshold comparisons are inclusive.

| Cohort label | Threshold | Context |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `default` | 7.0 | No higher-threshold cohort applies. |
| `esrd_epo` | 7.0 | EPO-managed renal anaemia alone does not raise the threshold. |
| `cardiac_surgery` | 7.5 | Qualifying cardiac procedure. |
| `ortho_surgery` | 8.0 | Qualifying orthopaedic operation, without requiring cardiac history. |
| `cardiopulmonary_comorbidity` | 8.0 | Qualifying heart-disease diagnoses. The stored name remains, but lung disease alone does not trigger it. |

`ortho_cardiac` is a deprecated label retained for historical records. The detector emits `ortho_surgery` for the current orthopaedic rule. `mtp`, `heme_malignancy_active`, and `cohort_unknown` have no numeric threshold. The classifier interprets their labels as well as their thresholds.

## Order-level exclusions and reservations

Before ordinary Hb classification, the default routing can identify these results:

- `RETURNED_NOT_TRANSFUSED` records an order whose units were all returned without transfusion.
- `PERIOP_TRANSFUSION_EXEMPT` records declared surgical use or type-and-screen. `BDVSTDT.USETYPE` codes `2` and `3` supply these declarations.

Both results are excluded from appropriateness scoring. An exemption does not assert that a transfusion was clinically indicated.

The separate MSBOS reservation screen compares reserved units with the planned operation's reference limit. An excess can produce `PREOP_OVER_RESERVATION`. Missing or ambiguous procedure evidence routes to review. The platelet reservation screen has its own procedure-specific count limits. These are reservation rules, distinct from the platelet transfusion indications below. An excluded order can retain a `reservation_annotation` without changing its classification.

## RBC decision order

<AlgorithmDecisionFlow />

This flow starts after order-level routing and shows the default Hb-present path. Missing Hb ordinarily produces `INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE`. An optional missing-Hb positive-evidence mode can apply Hb-independent evidence or defer to LLM review. That mode is not the default.

The universal `Hb ≤ 7.0` rule runs before MTP and unknown-cohort checks. MTP then clears the remaining matching orders. Unknown context at `Hb > 7.0` routes to review before the later bypasses.

A delta-Hb bypass requires a drop of at least 1.5 g/dL within 6 hours, 2.0 within 12 hours, or 2.5 within 24 hours. At higher cohort thresholds, crystalloid of at least 2 L in the prior 4 hours or an isolated low Hb without a prior trend can send a would-be clear to review. These checks do not undo the earlier universal rule. Vital signs alone do not create a deterministic bypass.

When the declared-use exemption is disabled, the legacy path can clear an order with a procedure in the preceding 6 hours. An upcoming procedure within 72 hours, or a surgical declaration, instead defers an above-threshold order to the LLM. An upcoming procedure does not automatically establish a transfusion indication.

## Hb evidence and audit population

The deterministic classifier uses the selected pre-order Hb from the seven-day lookup, with source and freshness metadata. The LLM's `SUB_THRESHOLD_HB` evidence uses the lowest Hb in the previous 24 hours, bounded by the latest transfusion in that window. These are separate values and must not be described as interchangeable.

The intended population is adult inpatients. The deployment must supply the approved adult cohort rather than assume that every export has already been filtered. Product routing separates RBC from platelets and other components.

## Platelet indications

The platelet path follows the Chula draft policy represented in `prompt_builder/system_prompt.py` and `platelet_guardrail/models.py`. The 10,000, 20,000, 50,000, and 80,000/µL boundaries are inclusive. The 100,000/µL indication boundaries are strict.

| Grounded indication | Count condition |
| --- | --- |
| Lumbar puncture | ≤ 50,000/µL in medicine or obstetrics, ≤ 80,000/µL in surgery or orthopaedics. |
| CVC insertion, thoracocentesis, arthrocentesis, or dental extraction | ≤ 50,000/µL. Bone marrow aspiration or biopsy does not qualify under this rule. |
| Major non-neuraxial surgery, planned or performed in the preceding 24 hours | ≤ 80,000/µL, or < 100,000/µL with high bleeding risk. |
| Consumptive thrombocytopenia or DIC without active bleeding | ≤ 10,000/µL. |
| Chemotherapy or HSCT without active bleeding | ≤ 10,000/µL, or a supported projection to ≤ 10,000/µL within 24 hours. |
| Acute, acute-on-chronic, growing, or operative intracranial bleed | < 100,000/µL. The acute window lasts seven days from the documenting note or imaging. |
| Stable, non-operative intracranial bleed | ≤ 50,000/µL. |
| Clinically significant active bleeding | ≤ 50,000/µL. |
| Aplastic anaemia on active therapy | ≤ 10,000/µL or a supported projection to that level within 24 hours. During ATG or with sepsis, ≤ 20,000/µL. |

The count projection is calculated from the last two usable pre-order counts. A standing order's target is not a count trend.

The prompt also names exclusion populations, including dengue without bleeding, TTP, HIT, ITP, snakebite without qualifying bleeding, and chronic marrow failure without a qualifying treatment context. A low count alone does not clear these cases. The condition-specific exceptions must be checked against the full policy.

In LLM review, a diagnosis code alone does not ground an indication or exclusion. The deterministic prophylaxis rule is a deliberate exception. When enabled, it can clear a fresh count ≤ 10,000/µL with qualifying haematology, chemotherapy, or transplant codes and no matching exclusion code. Some codes require separate chemotherapy evidence. This rule is enabled by default. Platelet LLM review remains disabled by default, which can leave an audit incomplete as described in [What this is](/blood_audit/en/clinical/what-this-is/).

## Policy authority and validation

The hospital's approved policy and clinical review govern use of these findings. This source comparison verifies what the software implements. It does not validate the medical policy, model accuracy, or a production deployment.
