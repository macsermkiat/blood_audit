blood_audit reviews adult inpatient red blood cell (RBC) and platelet orders from HOSxP exports at Chulalongkorn Hospital. It combines laboratory results, diagnoses, procedures, medication records, and clinical notes. The audit records a result and its evidence for later quality improvement review.

## How an order is reviewed

The deterministic classifier applies the configured policy first. Some orders finish at this stage. Orders that need interpretation can continue to an LLM, subject to privacy admission and evidence checks. A cohort is a per-order clinical-context label used to select an RBC threshold. It is not a permanent patient group.

The core classifications are `APPROPRIATE`, `INAPPROPRIATE`, `NEEDS_REVIEW`, `INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE`, and `POTENTIALLY_INAPPROPRIATE`. Routing results also identify returned units, perioperative exemptions, and reservation findings. [Policy summary](/blood_audit/en/clinical/policy-summary/) describes these rules and their limits.

Platelet LLM review is disabled by default. With that path disabled, the pipeline omits unresolved platelet rows. The application checks for missing results and rejects an incomplete audit rather than treating those orders as reviewed. A deployment must resolve this configuration before using a cohort that includes such orders.

## Evidence checks and human decisions

The LLM path checks quoted evidence and applies guardrails before storing a result. A grounding failure retains the case for review, commonly with `review_reason = "hallucination_suspect"`. These checks reduce specific failure modes. They do not establish clinical accuracy or replace clinician validation.

The reviewer dashboard shows committed RBC and platelet cases whose classification or review flag requires human review. Reviewers can agree, override with a reason, or escalate. The dashboard keeps decision history tied to the exact audit row and version. It requires a trusted identity adapter for deployed authentication.

## Outputs and scope

The monthly report contains RBC aggregates and separate physician own-view files. Platelet rows do not enter RBC rates. [Report output](/blood_audit/en/clinical/report-output/) explains the files and scoring exclusions.

The application supports retrospective quality improvement. It does not authorize bedside transfusions or send real-time treatment alerts. Deployment, clinical validation, and institutional approval are separate from the presence of implemented software checks.
