Scope control

The first safety check is eligibility

Before judging appropriateness, the app narrows the dataset to the population where the policy applies.

Included

audit scope
  • Adult inpatient transfusion orders.
  • Eligible RBC products: LPRC, LDPRC, SDR.
  • Completed in-house requests with admission context.

Excluded

not judged
  • Pediatric, outpatient, cancelled, refused, or inter-hospital rows.
  • Non-RBC products.
  • PR 17.2 out-of-scope diagnoses, such as hemoglobinopathy, AIHA, TMA, and obstetric conditions.
Out of scope is not inappropriateSlide 5

Hb anchor

How the app chooses the Hb used for audit

Lookback
< 7d

Uses the most recent valid pre-transfusion Hb before the order. If none exists, the row becomes INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE.

Preference
Lab

Hematology Hb is preferred over POCT when available. Same-time ties use the later inserted lab item.

Trend
6/12/24h

A rapid Hb fall can trigger an acute blood-loss bypass when the drop meets the clinical window threshold.

Important distinction: missing Hb is a documentation gap. It is retained for QI visibility, not counted as inappropriate.

Clinical context

Cohort means audit context, not a permanent patient label

Default / ESRDDefault and ESRD-EPO contexts use Hb ≤ 7.0 g/dL.
Cardiac surgeryRecent cardiac or large-vessel operative context. Threshold: ≤ 7.5.
Orthopedic surgeryAn orthopedic operation is sufficient for the cohort. Threshold: ≤ 8.0.
Heart diseaseHeart-disease diagnoses trigger the retained cardiopulmonary cohort label. Threshold: ≤ 8.0.
MTPMassive-transfusion pattern. Bypasses Hb threshold as appropriate.
Active heme malignancyHeme diagnosis + recent chemo + ANC < 500. Routes to review.

For Hb above 7.0, unavailable procedure data routes to review rather than assuming the default cohort.

Decision tree

First matching rule wins

1. Complete returns?Routing exemption if supported and not contradicted
2. Declared surgical use?Configured order-level exemption
3. Missing Hb?INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE by default; opt-in evidence route differs
4. Hb ≤ 7.0?APPROPRIATE before cohort review
5. MTP?APPROPRIATE
6. Unknown context?NEEDS_REVIEW for remaining Hb > 7.0
7. Legacy procedure route?6h bypass or 72h review deferral only when declared-use exemption is off
8. Acute Hb drop?APPROPRIATE when the configured delta-Hb rule fires
9. No numeric threshold?NEEDS_REVIEW
10. Hb ≤ cohort floor?APPROPRIATE unless remaining safety flags require review
11. Above floor, Hb < 10?NEEDS_REVIEW
12. Hb ≥ 10?POTENTIALLY_INAPPROPRIATE; review eligible evidence

Hb tier

Universal low-Hb rule first, then cohort tiers

Tier 1
≤ threshold

Hb ≤ 7.0 is APPROPRIATE before cohort review. At higher cohort floors, safety flags can require review.

Tier 2
threshold < Hb < 10

NEEDS_REVIEW. The gray zone is not automatically called appropriate or inappropriate.

Tier 3
≥ 10

POTENTIALLY_INAPPROPRIATE, then reviewed for documented positive evidence.

Safety gates

Review routes after earlier rules

These checks apply to remaining Hb above 7.0 after earlier exits. They do not override the universal low-Hb rule.

A
Unknown procedure context
For remaining Hb above 7.0, missing procedure data routes to review rather than a default cohort.
B
Hemodilution signal
7.0 < Hb ≤ cohort threshold, with at least 2 L crystalloid in the prior 4 hours.
C
Single low Hb without trend
7.0 < Hb ≤ 8.0, within the cohort floor, without a prior 24-hour comparator.
D
Special or gray-zone context
Remaining active heme, above-threshold Hb below 10, and high-Hb rows route to evidence review when no earlier rule applies.
Universal Hb ≤ 7.0 takes precedence over these review checksSlide 10

LLM route

AI is used only for evidence review, then checked fail-closed

Model submission requires signed admission for the exact redacted evidence. The verifier checks citations against that evidence and stops at the first failure.

Normalized textNFC normalization gives quotes and sources a shared text representation.
Contiguous matchThe quote must match word-aligned source text.
Cited sourceThe evidence ID must identify one supplied source.
Unique within sourceThe normalized quote must have one aligned match.
Minimum lengthThe production minimum is 25 normalized characters.
Lab tuple integritySupported analyte, value, and unit triples must match the quote and source.

Fail-closed rule: if no attempt passes grounding, the pipeline stores NEEDS_REVIEW with review reason hallucination_suspect. A later passing attempt can supersede an earlier rejection.

Grounding checks establish citation support, not clinical truth or de-identification qualitySlide 11

Clinical sign-off checklist

The review questions for clinicians

1
Are the thresholds clinically correct?Default, cardiac surgery, orthopedic surgery, heart disease, ESRD-EPO, MTP, and active heme routing.
2
Are the bypasses acceptable?MTP, hard perioperative evidence, delta-Hb, and the separately configured legacy procedure routes.
3
Are review flags conservative enough?Unknown context, hemodilution, single-low-Hb-no-trend, gray-zone Hb, and high Hb.
4
Are exclusions appropriate?Age, product, status, inpatient scope, referral scope, and diagnosis exclusions.
5
Is the report clinically useful?Does it support QI discussion without becoming punitive or real-time CDS?
6
What needs local policy wording?Confirm terms that should match internal KCMH language before pilot use.
